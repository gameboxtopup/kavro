Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Information

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Kavro, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, contact details, messages, and information submitted through forms. We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and approximate usage statistics to improve our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect may be used to respond to inquiries, improve our services, communicate important updates, provide customer support, analyze website performance, and protect our platform against abuse or unauthorized activity.

3. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, improve user experience, measure website performance, and understand visitor behavior. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, although some website features may not function properly.

4. Third-Party Services

Kavro may use trusted third-party services to provide website functionality, analytics, hosting, authentication, payment processing, or affiliate tracking. These services operate under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them before using their services.

5. Affiliate Disclosure

Some pages may contain affiliate links. If you purchase a product or service through an affiliate link, Kavro may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate partnerships do not influence our commitment to providing honest and useful information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect the information entrusted to us. While no website or online service can guarantee absolute security, we continually work to maintain a safe environment for our visitors and customers.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary to operate our services, comply with legal obligations, protect our rights, or work with trusted service providers that support our business operations.

8. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill legitimate business purposes, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, or improve our services.
